### Meta Reasoning Task Guide

#### Problem Understanding:
- Objective: Identify the goal or desired outcome.
- Comprehend the problem's nature and scope.

#### Reasoning Process Options:
Choose the most relevant approaches based on the problem complexity.

1. **Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning**:
   - Break down the problem into intermediate steps.
   - Example:
     - Question: What is the gross margin decline percent in fiscal 2004 from 2003?
     - Steps:
       1. Extract gross margin for 2003: 27.5%
       2. Extract gross margin for 2004: 27.3%
       3. Calculate the difference: 0.2%

2. **Evidence Extraction**:
   - Extract relevant data and details from the provided context.
   - Example:
     - Context: Table showing net sales, cost of sales, and gross margin for 2004-2002.
     - Extracted Evidence: Gross margin percentages for 2003 and 2004.

3. **Decomposition**:
   - Break down the problem into sub-problems and solve each part.
   - Example:
     - Sub-questions:
       1. What was the gross margin in 2003?
       2. What was the gross margin in 2004?
       3. What is the difference between these values?

#### Final Answer:
- Summarize findings and present the final answer.
- If possible, provide answers in both percent and decimal notation.
- Example: Final Answer: 0.2%, 0.002

### Simplified Example

```
Context:
The table shows net sales, cost of sales, and gross margin for 2004-2002.

Question:
What was the gross margin decline percent in fiscal 2004 from 2003?

Chosen Approach:
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning

Steps:
1. Gross margin for 2003: 27.5%
2. Gross margin for 2004: 27.3%
3. Difference: 0.2%

Final Answer:
0.2%, 0.002
```

### Example 1

Context:
The table shows the total revenue and net profit for XYZ Corporation for the years 2017, 2018, and 2019.

Year | Total Revenue | Net Profit
---- | --------------| ----------
2017 | $500,000      | $50,000
2018 | $550,000      | $55,000
2019 | $600,000      | $60,000

Question:
What was the percentage change in total revenue from 2017 to 2018?

**Chosen Approach**:
Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning

**Steps**:
1. Total revenue for 2017: $500,000
2. Total revenue for 2018: $550,000
3. Difference: $550,000 - $500,000 = $50,000
4. Percentage change: ($50,000 / $500,000) * 100 = 10%

**Final Answer**:
10%, 0.1, .1

### Example 2

Context:
The table shows the expenses and net income for ABC Corporation for the years 2019 and 2020.

Category | 2019 | 2020
---- | ---- | ----
Expenses | $300,000 | $350,000
Net Income | $200,000 | $250,000

Question:
What was the percentage change in net income from 2019 to 2020?

**Chosen Approach**:
Decomposition

**Sub-questions**:
1. What was the net income in 2019? (sq1)
2. What was the net income in 2020? (sq2)
3. What is the difference in net income between 2019 and 2020? (sq3)
4. What is the percentage change in net income from 2019 to 2020? (sq4)

**Answers**:
1. The net income in 2019 was $200,000. (Answer to sq1)
2. The net income in 2020 was $250,000. (Answer to sq2)
3. The difference in net income is $250,000 - $200,000 = $50,000. (Answer to sq3)
4. The percentage change is ($50,000 / $200,000) * 100 = 25%. (Answer to sq4)

**Final Answer**:
25%, 0.25, .25

### Example 4

Context:
The table shows the revenues, expenses, and net income for PQR Corporation for the years 2017, 2018, and 2019.

Year | Revenue | Expenses | Net Income
---- | --------| -------- | ----------
2017 | $400,000| $250,000 | $150,000
2018 | $450,000| $270,000 | $180,000
2019 | $500,000| $300,000 | $200,000

Question:
What was the average percentage change in net income over the years 2017, 2018, and 2019?

**Chosen Approach**:
Decomposition and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) Reasoning

**Sub-questions**:
1. What was the net income in 2017? (sq1)
2. What was the net income in 2018? (sq2)
3. What was the net income in 2019? (sq3)
4. What is the percentage change in net income from 2017 to 2018? (sq4)
5. What is the percentage change in net income from 2018 to 2019? (sq5)
6. What is the average percentage change over these years? (sq6)

**Answers**:
1. Net income in 2017: $150,000 (Answer to sq1)
2. Net income in 2018: $180,000 (Answer to sq2)
3. Net income in 2019: $200,000 (Answer to sq3)
4. Percentage change from 2017 to 2018: ($180,000 - $150,000) / $150,000 * 100 = 20% (Answer to sq4)
5. Percentage change from 2018 to 2019: ($200,000 - $180,000) / $180,000 * 100 = 11.11% (Answer to sq5)
6. Average percentage change: (20 + 11.11) / 2 = 15.56% (Answer to sq6)

**Final Answer**:
The average percentage change in net income over the years 2017, 2018, and 2019 is approximately 15.56% (0.1556 or .156).
