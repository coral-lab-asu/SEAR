- Bobby Hull (CAN) is a Canadian former ice hockey player who is regarded as one of the greatest players of all time, known for his blonde hair and legendary skating speed.
- Shane Doan (CAN) is a Canadian former professional ice hockey forward who spent the entirety of his NHL career with the Winnipeg Jets/Arizona Coyotes franchise, retiring in 2017. He was the longest-serving NHL captain at the time of his retirement.

**Question:** Arizona Coyotes player number 19 played in how many NHL seasons?

**Optimal Approach:**

**Problem Understanding:**
1. Determine the Objective: Identify how many NHL seasons the Arizona Coyotes player number 19 played.
2. Understand the Problem: This requires extracting the relevant row from the table based on the player number and calculating the number of seasons from the career span.

**Reasoning Process:**
3. Extract Relevant Information:
   - Player number 19: Shane Doan, Career: 1996-2017

4. Python Code:
```python
# Extract the row with player number 19
player_number = 19

# List of players
players = [
    dict(No=7, Player='Keith Tkachuk', Position='C', Career='1992-2001', Date_honored='December 23, 2011'),
    dict(No=9, Player='Bobby Hull', Position='LW', Career='1972-1980', Date_honored='February 19, 1989'),
    dict(No=19, Player='Shane Doan', Position='RW', Career='1996-2017', Date_honored='February 24, 2019')
]

# Find the player with number 19
player = next(p for p in players if p['No'] == player_number)

# Extract the career span from the player
career_span = player['Career']

# Calculate the number of seasons
start_year, end_year = map(int, career_span.split('-'))
number_of_seasons = end_year - start_year + 1

print(number_of_seasons)
#DONE
```

**Final Answer:** Shane Doan played in the NHL for 21 seasons.

**Problem Understanding:**
