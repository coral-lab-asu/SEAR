**Reasoning Process (Think Step-by-Step, Exploring Thoughts):**

Begin by analyzing the question and the provided data. Follow these stages, generating multiple thoughts for each, evaluating them, and choosing the most promising path. Your output should detail this step-by-step reasoning.

**Stage 1: Problem Understanding & Initial Decomposition**

  * **Objective:** What is the core information or calculation the question is asking for? What are the key entities and temporal constraints (e.g., specific years, durations, order of events)?
  * **Initial Data Scan:** What tables/sections/columns seem most relevant? Are there explicit temporal references, numerical values, or categorical data that directly relate to the question?
  * **Decomposition (Thought Generation):** Brainstorm 2-3 distinct ways to break down this problem. Consider:
      * **Thought 1 (Direct Lookup/Extraction):** Can the answer be found directly or with minimal extraction from the table/text, especially concerning temporal information (e.g., specific year's data, event name)?
      * **Thought 2 (Simple Calculation/Comparison):** Does it involve a straightforward arithmetic operation (sum, difference, percentage change over time) or a direct temporal comparison (e.g., earlier/later, highest/lowest in a period)?
      * **Thought 3 (Temporal Sequencing/Relational Analysis):** Does it require establishing a sequence of events, inferring implicit temporal relationships, or understanding intervals (e.g., career duration, multi-year events)?
      * **Thought 4 (Multi-Table/Textual Integration):** Does it require combining information from multiple tables or unstructured text for temporal context or a comprehensive answer?
  * **Evaluation (Thought Selection):** Evaluate each thought's potential. Rank them by confidence/relevance (e.g., "High," "Medium," "Low") and briefly explain why. Select the **Most Promising Initial Thought(s)** to proceed.

**Stage 2: Detailed Plan Generation based on Selected Thought(s)**

  * **Refinement:** Based on your chosen initial thought(s), elaborate on the precise steps needed to extract temporal information and build the answer.
  * **Sub-Steps (Thought Generation):** Generate 2-3 alternative concrete sub-steps or operations for the chosen path.
      * **For Direct Lookup/Extraction (Temporal):**
          * *Sub-Thought A:* Specify the exact row(s) and column(s) containing the target year/period and associated entity/event.
          * *Sub-Thought B:* If multiple potential temporal matches, how to filter/prioritize (e.g., "latest," "earliest," specific year)?
      * **For Calculation/Comparison (Temporal):**
          * *Sub-Thought A:* Identify all relevant numerical values and their associated temporal markers (e.g., values for 2014 and 2015).
          * *Sub-Thought B:* Define the exact mathematical or comparative operation required (e.g., subtraction for net change, finding minimum value within a year range).
          * *Sub-Thought C:* Specify how to handle units or aggregation if necessary.
      * **For Temporal Sequencing/Relational Analysis:**
          * *Sub-Thought A:* Outline how to extract all relevant events and their associated timestamps/periods.
          * *Sub-Thought B:* Describe the method for ordering these events chronologically or for identifying specific temporal overlaps/gaps.
          * *Sub-Thought C:* How to pinpoint the specific temporal interval or event that answers the question (e.g., "time period with no sponsor").
      * **For Multi-Table/Textual Integration:**
          * *Sub-Thought A:* Detail how to identify and extract relevant pieces of information from each source (table and text).
          * *Sub-Thought B:* Explain how to cross-reference or combine these pieces of information, paying attention to temporal consistency.
  * **Evaluation (Sub-Thought Selection):** For each sub-step, evaluate its feasibility and efficiency. Select the **Most Promising Detailed Plan**.

**Stage 3: Execution & Answer Derivation**

  * **Execution Steps:** Carry out the detailed plan you've selected. For each step, clearly state the action taken and the result.
      * **Data Extraction:** Clearly show what data (including temporal details) is extracted and from where.
          * *Example (FinQA):* "Extracted '2014 net revenue' as $5735 million and '2015 net revenue' as $5829 million."
          * *Example (HiTab):* "Extracted 'Women (Non-University or University Postsecondary Diploma)' percentages and 'Men (Non-University or University Postsecondary Diploma)' percentages for all countries."
          * *Example (WikiTableQuestions):* "Extracted the 'Murdered' row values and their corresponding 'Year' columns."
      * **Calculations/Inference:** Show any calculations performed or logical inferences made.
          * *Example (FinQA):* "Calculation: $5829 - $5735 = $94."
          * *Example (Squall):* "Filter years less than 2003: 1963, 1967, 1971, 1975, 1977, 1981, 1985, 1987, 1990, 1995, 1999. Identify '\# of votes' for these years and find the minimum."
          * *Example (HybridQA):* "Identify Shane Doan's career span from the 'Career' column: 1996-2017. Calculate the number of seasons: 2017 - 1996 + 1 = 22 seasons."
      * **Intermediate Results:** State any intermediate conclusions drawn. This will form the narrative of your reasoning.
  * **Final Answer Formulation:** Synthesize the results to form the final answer to the original question. Ensure it is clear, concise, and directly addresses the question.
  * **Confidence Check:** Briefly assess your confidence in the final answer based on the thoroughness and support of the reasoning path taken (e.g., "High confidence due to direct extraction and simple arithmetic," "Medium confidence due to multiple inferential steps").
